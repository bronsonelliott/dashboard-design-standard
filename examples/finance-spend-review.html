The brief

AudienceFP&A and budget owners, during and just after month-end close.
DecisionWhich budget owners need a spend conversation this month, and whether the full-year forecast needs a change.
TypeAnalytical monitoring: a fixed starting view for a recurring diagnostic question (section 03).
ComparisonsAnnual plan for status. Latest forecast for the full-year outlook.
CutoffsSpend over plan: Within limit up to 2%, Watch 2–5%, Act over 5%. Owned by the FP&A lead.

How this applies the standard

  • Total spend, OpEx and COGS share one definition each, written in the footer (MEA-01, TXT-03).
  • Actuals, forecast and plan look different: solid bars, dashed bars, ticks (VIS-03, DSP-06).
  • Higher-is-worse status is spelled out, with shape and word (MEA-04, COL-02).
  • The exceptions list answers "who do I call" without a click (INT-01, MEA-05).
  • Data-as-of date and close date sit in the header (TXT-03).
  • Variance by category is a signed bar chart, not a waterfall of floating bars (VIS-01, DSP-15).

About the data

  • A made-up mid-size software company with about $420M of planned annual spend.
  • Every number is invented, but they add up: departments sum to operating expense, components to COGS, and both to total spend.
