The brief

AudienceSales leadership and revenue operations, in the annual planning review.
DecisionWhere to tighten discount policy, and which products and regions need a margin plan for next year.
TypeStrategic: a periodic review of outcomes against the prior year (section 03).
ComparisonsPrior year. The dataset has no targets, so none are invented.
CutoffsProduct margin: Within limit at 10% or more, Watch from 0% to 10%, Act below 0%.

How this applies the standard

  • One screen, eight items, most important top left (LAY-01, LAY-03, LAY-06).
  • Every number has a comparison: 2024 on each KPI and in each table row (MEA-01).
  • Titles state the finding, computed from the data (TXT-01).
  • Bars start at zero; signed bars share one zero line (VIS-01, DSP-15).
  • Status shows a shape and a word, not color alone, with cutoffs written down (COL-02, MEA-04).
  • Muted bars, one highlight color for 2025 (COL-01).
  • No pie for region share, no gauge for margin (AVD-01).

About the data

  • Built from a public SaaS sales sample dataset: about 10,000 order lines from 2022 to 2025 for invented companies.
  • Months are by order date. Discount bands use each line's discount rate.
  • Every figure on the screen is computed from that dataset. Nothing is hand-entered.
